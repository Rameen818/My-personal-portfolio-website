The Trick Behind Responsive Image & Text Left-Right In Card

At the first, I was also confused about how to make image & text cards responsive and how to adjust image . After practicing, I understood the real trick......

First, give the cards fixed height and width in pixels for desktop,but on the small screen decrease the width and adjust the height according to the content length, then use overflow:hidden to hide unneccessary content, then use flex just on desktop and medium screen and on small screen adjust content in column, to adjust the image: give the full width to the image on all screens ,and give full height just on the large screen but reduce the height of image on small screen and then use object-fit:cover ; to cover the image but it may crop it but by using the object-position you can show the aspect of the image that you wants. Just by using these techniques , you can make cards responsive easily.
